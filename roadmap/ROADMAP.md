# Product Roadmap — Holistic Wellness

Internal working roadmap for the app concept. Cleaned up from the `proposed_roadmap` Google Sheet; the same rows are in [`roadmap.csv`](roadmap.csv) for re-importing into a spreadsheet.

**Guiding principle:** we're essentially starting a school for *how to live*. Plans need to be precise and every journey needs clean, intuitive UI/UX. Longer term, this could become part of academic and school life in general.

Owner, status and target date haven't been set for any item yet, so they're left out of the tables below (the columns are kept in the CSV). Size is Small / Medium / Large.

---

## 🟢 Now — high confidence (0–6 weeks)

### Daily experience

| Initiative | Why | Size | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI guide (chatbot)** — helps users find content, ask questions and design a personalised journey | Personalisation and content discovery | Large | Time spent, successful bot responses, people returning to the daily calendar it builds |
